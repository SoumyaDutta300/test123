The model missing from the market's menu.

Payments are moving inside software. Businesses no longer choose an acquirer and bolt on software — they choose software, and payments come with it. Processing has become a commodity; the value now sits with whoever owns the merchant relationship and lifecycle. Which leaves every institution and platform a three-way choice:

1. Build

"Own it, eventually"

Building acquiring infrastructure in-house typically takes three to five years and an eight-figure budget — ownership arrives after the market has moved.

2. Rent

"Fast, but never yours"

PayFac-as-a-Service, managed programs, referral agreements — every rented model closes the capability gap while the merchant relationship and the economics stay with the provider.

3. License & Own

"Fast, and yours"

License the acquiring and PayFac operating platform and run it as your own program — the option most market taxonomies don't yet have a row for. Payabbhi® is that row.
